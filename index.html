Katha Studio

Katha Studio is a personal tool that its creator runs on their own computer to make short story videos and publish them to their own channels, such as Socha Kyaa on YouTube.

When the creator connects their YouTube channel, Katha Studio uses the YouTube Data API to upload the videos they choose, with the title, description, tags and thumbnail they review first, and to read the channel's name. It is not a public service and has no other users.
